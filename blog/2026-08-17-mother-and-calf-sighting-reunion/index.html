17 August 2026Off Réunion, aboard the Lady La Fee

Mother and calf sighting Reunion

Humpback whalesRéunionAbyss

The first humpback whale sighting of the mission, from the beautiful Lady La Fee: a mother–calf pair.

The sighting was made with the ABYSS team, whose knowledge of these waters and of the whales made it possible. Together with Olivier Adam, Lucie Jean-Labadye, Bertrand Denis, Beverley Ecalle, Yann Doh, Nadège Gandilhon and Anne Barat.

All photographs in this entry are by Nadège Gandilhon (ABYSS) and are reproduced with her kind permission. © Nadège Gandilhon.
